AIR DEFAME 101 — v0.7 Dollars and Battle Points

Request: add an economy first, using Dollars and BP (Battle Points = XP),
with a tech tree to follow in a future update.

Both balances start at zero and accumulate separately. Dollars will fund
future equipment purchases. BP is earned experience for future research.
No purchases, research nodes, ammunition charges or repair charges exist yet.

Initial reward table:
Aircraft destroyed:                $250 + 100 BP
Incoming missile intercepted:      $150 +  75 BP
Mission victory:                   $500 + 250 BP
Each base hit remaining on victory: $100 +  50 BP

Radar and IR interceptions receive the same rewards. The actual target class
sets the reward; uncertain radar identification does not alter it. A confirmed
interception pays once. Flares, misses, aircraft escapes, and enemy missiles
hitting terrain or the base do not award interception rewards.

Combat rewards save when earned. A defeat, voluntary restart, or return to
the menu keeps those rewards. Victory bonuses are awarded once, at successful
mission completion. Reopening/redrawing the results screen does not pay again.
After app termination the incomplete mission is marked INTERRUPTED, and already
saved combat rewards remain. The mission itself does not resume.

UI:
- Menu cards and the gameplay top bar show Dollars and BP.
- ECONOMY / REWARDS opens the reward table, last-mission receipt, lifetime
  earnings, wins, and completed missions.
- Mission results show separate aircraft, missile, victory, and base bonuses.
- A failed save displays SAVE PENDING with RETRY SAVE in the economy screen.
  A new mission cannot overwrite unsaved earnings. Backgrounding also retries.

Persistence:
The Android preference transaction saves both balances, mission receipt, and
credited target IDs together. Duplicate checks exist at target, mission-event,
and mission-settlement levels. The existing best score remains separate.
The existing Android package/signing identity is retained for in-place updates.
Progress is local to the device and survives normal app restarts and updates.
Uninstalling or clearing app data removes it. No historical score conversion
is performed: scores before v0.7 did not contain the required reward history.

Engineering checks:
EconomyTest covers rates, victory/defeat/withdrawal, recovery, duplicate payouts,
failed-write retries, actual radar and IR interception credit, and integer
bounds. Existing model, IR, missile-interception and update tests pass. Desktop
Android stubs exercise preference persistence, preservation of the old best
score, receipt redraws, save failure/retry, and the actual drawing code.
The APK compiles and its signing verifies. Device installation/playtesting is
still required; desktop stubs do not verify Android filesystem durability.
