AIR DEFENSE v0.17 — sensor and guidance rebuild

Based on AIR_DEFENSE_Rebuild_Design_Notes.md, retained as REBUILD-NOTES.md.
Built on v0.16; menus, Mission 1, cinematic, music, currency, research and save keys are retained.
Work branch: radar-rebuild-v0.17. The main branch retains v0.16 for comparison.

Operator flow
1. PLAY > STORY > BEFORE THE DAWN > LOADOUT.
2. Choose Rampart/Stonebolt (semi-active), Sentinel (active radar), or Ember (IR).
3. Deploy. The existing introduction finishes or skips directly to radar gameplay.
4. Detection supplies last measured position/range. Tracking slots are automatic.
5. Wait for a stable track before a radar engagement. PRIORITIZE requests a slot.
6. Semi-active: LOCK and maintain illumination. Active radar: FIRE from a stable track.
7. IR: cue and acquire the missile's own seeker, then FIRE; battery IRST is not required.

Radar states
ACQUIRING: observations are still establishing a motion estimate.
STABLE: detailed estimated information and movement prediction are available.
COASTING: update missed; clearly aged estimates, no new radar firing solution.
LOST: track expired; slot released, last-known plot briefly remains.
Identity colors remain separate from tracking/lock symbols.

Capacity
Watchpost keeps 5 tracks, 30 km detection / 24 km tracking / 16 km locking and scan 1.00.
It now has independent 2-target illumination and 2-missile midcourse support pools.
Multiple semi-active missiles may share illumination of one target; supported missile cap is 6.
Active missiles consume one midcourse slot each, even against the same target.
Acquisition, missile termination or explicit release frees their slot. No silent overflow eviction.

Guidance
Semi-active support loss follows the last estimate during a short recovery window.
Active stages distinguish MIDCOURSE, SEARCHING and ACTIVE - TARGET ACQUIRED.
Searching alone is not successful acquisition. Seekers have limited range, cone and time.
IR missiles require seeker lock before launch and can lose/reacquire or be distracted by flares.
All families use the selected launcher's ready ammunition and normal reload system.
Only the selected engagement's guidance lines are shown, with an operator toggle.

Aircraft awareness
Automatic TWS allocation does not notify aircraft. Fictional receiver profiles govern
search, fire-control, active-seeker, optical/visual warning availability. Skill and delay
influence reactions; a contact without suitable equipment cannot read hidden player actions.

Playtest equipment
MIM-303 Sentinel and FIM-306 Ember are free prototype options for testing the new families.
Their characteristics are fictional initial balance. Stonebolt research and purchase prices
are unchanged. Equip one family before deployment; it supplies all three launchers.
Watchpost has no IRST. Standalone IRST search and IRST-only unknown-range plots are future work.
Optional retargeting and overflow support eviction are disabled; no lock change redirects a missile.

Install over the existing AIR DEFENSE app to preserve progress. The signing identity is unchanged.
Active missions are not restored after process termination. Hardware/touch/audio and balance
still require testing on a real phone; desktop stubs are not an Android emulator.
