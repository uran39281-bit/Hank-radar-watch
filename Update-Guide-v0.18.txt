AIR DEFENSE v0.18 — easier Mission 1

BEFORE THE DAWN now uses Rookie / Smart Level 1 for every aircraft.
Previously the mission randomly assigned Smart Levels 1 through 5.

Rookie behavior:
- Lower warning recognition: 28% for a compatible radar-lock warning.
- Slower reaction: approximately 5 seconds to a recognized radar lock, with timing variation.
- More likely to continue an attack pass and less likely to evade.
- No advanced notch maneuver or notch-related guidance break below Smart Level 3.
- Rookie approaches stay at least 1,800 m above local terrain and skip low evasive dives.
- No learned increase in caution at Smart Level 1.

Fixed a separate collision bug: radar missiles previously detonated as a weak graze while
still approaching an aircraft, before they could reach direct-hit distance. Graze damage now
occurs only after closest approach. Direct hits can destroy moving, piloted aircraft normally.

Aircraft and missiles retain their existing physical characteristics. Radar, player weapons,
ammunition, mission roster, rewards, research and saves are unchanged.
Stronger AI remains available for later missions. Developers can adjust mission1.smartLevel
from 1 through 5 in assets/combat.properties before rebuilding.

Install over v0.17 to preserve local progress. Restart Mission 1 to use the new rookie profile.
Model tests check assignment, warning delays and meaningful engagement behavior.
Final difficulty still needs player feedback on a real phone.
