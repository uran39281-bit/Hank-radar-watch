AIR DEFAME 101 — v0.9 USA tech tree

Requested: a War Thunder-style tech tree with USA as the only nation, replacing
the default player battery and missiles with the following fictional equipment.

ADS-201 Watchpost
Detection: 30 km
Tracking: 24 km
Radar lock: 16 km
Simultaneous tracks: 5
Datalink channels: 2
Radar scan speed: 1.00 (on the existing 1.00–10.00 scale)
IR guidance and IR lock range: N/A

MIM-301 Rampart
Radar / semi-active homing
Maximum guidance: 35 seconds
Maximum speed: 2,400 km/h
Maximum G: 14
Mass: 140 kg
Maximum AOA: 12 degrees
Maximum thrust: 18 kN (18,000 N)

FIM-352 Stonebolt
Radar / semi-active homing
Maximum guidance: 40 seconds
Maximum speed: 2,700 km/h
Maximum G: 12
Mass: 180 kg
Maximum AOA: 10 degrees
Maximum thrust: 22 kN (22,000 N)

Implementation choices where values were not supplied:
- Watchpost and Rampart are free Rank I starters.
- Stonebolt follows Rampart: 600 BP research and $1,500 purchase.
- Research consumes only available banked BP up to the remaining requirement;
  partial progress persists. Purchase and equip are separate steps.
- One equipped missile type supplies all L1–L3 launchers for the next mission.
- Existing ammunition, free reloads, rewards, and enemy loadouts remain.
- Scan speed uses the existing 11 minus rating formula: 1.00 = ten-second sweep.
- Supporting arcade defaults: Rampart motor 5s / path 24km, Stonebolt motor 6s /
  path 28km; launch ceiling 13,700m. Watchpost still limits locks to 16km.
- Former HAWK/MIM-23/IR-6 player defaults are removed from the shipped loadout.
  The reusable IR engine remains for future compatible systems; this battery
  has no IR inventory, weapon toggle, or enabled heat seeker.

Progress compatibility:
Existing Dollars, BP, best score, lifetime earnings and mission receipts survive
in-place updates. Starter ownership is granted without charging old balances.
Research, ownership, selected missile, and currency save together. Failed
research/purchase/equip writes leave the previous in-memory transaction state;
retry is required. Duplicate research/purchases do not charge twice. Loadout
changes and spending are blocked during a mission.
