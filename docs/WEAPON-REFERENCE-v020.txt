AIR DEFENSE v0.20 weapon reference catalogue

Source: the user-supplied Aircraft, RWR and AI Update guide, retrieved 5 October 2026:
https://docs.google.com/document/d/1aUPYVPOLXG5LDyRoFjqV96M9hksr2b8GoINwVMx-Iwo/edit

WEAPON-REFERENCE-v020.csv transcribes the guide's reference tables. The catalogue
does not activate any weapon, change a player's loadout, enable WP-2/WP-3, or
create hostile missile spawns. Only aircraft.properties configures active fixed
WP-0/WP-1 aircraft presets. OFAB-100 is the provisional beginner bomb choice.

NOT_LISTED is unknown, never zero or unlimited. No checked weapon panel supplied
maximum aircraft release/launch speed. The aircraft preset release limits are
separately documented gameplay defaults, not reference weapon speed limits.

Missile maximum speed remains in Mach. Atmospheric conditions would be required
for a conversion to km/h. Launch range is the listed panel value, not guaranteed
effective range or maximum travel distance. Guidance lifetime is not motor burn
time. Bomb speed/travel follow release conditions; guidance/range are N/A.

Variant assumptions and guidance interpretation:
- KH-58SHK is assumed to mean Kh-58UShK pending confirmation of the suffix.
- Kh-58U A-prime/B/C share the listed flight values, with bands I/F/D respectively.
- Kh-31PD and Kh-58UShK list band D; radar AAMs and Kh-59MK list I. These are
  source-game labels, not universal real equipment frequency specifications.
- ARM panels use SARH labels, but the guide's developer reference describes
  passive radar-emitter homing. Catalogue guidance is passive anti-radiation.
- Kh-29TD uses the current guide's TV label, not its cited archived TV+IR label.
- Kh-59MK is active radar + inertial anti-ship; it is not the TV-guided Kh-59M.
- R-27R/T/ER/ET are distinct weapons, not one merged R-27 entry.
- FAB-500M/FAB-3000M need suffixes. FAB-3000M here is unguided, not UMPK.
- Explosive filler and TNT-equivalent mass are separate values; do not substitute
  the entire weapon mass for its explosive charge.
