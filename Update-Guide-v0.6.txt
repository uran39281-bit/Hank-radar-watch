AIR DEFAME 101 — v0.6 infrared launcher

User request: add an IR system for IR-guided missiles, inspired by War Thunder.

IR-6 is a fictional separate short-range battery. MIM-23 remains radar guided.
Use WEAPON on the right to select IR-6. Select a detected contact, TRACK it,
then IR LOCK / ACTIVATE. The seeker cools for 2 simulation seconds and needs
1.2 seconds of continuous usable heat to lock. FIRE IR-6 then releases the
launcher seeker; the missile guides itself. Selecting another contact or
switching weapon does not affect IR missiles already in flight. IR locks use
no radar illumination channels and do not trigger a radar-lock reaction.

Gameplay limits: 6–9 km acquisition for aircraft depending on aspect, 9 km
maximum slant launch range, 600 m minimum launch range, 6,000 m ceiling.
Rear exhaust is stronger; terrain blocks heat. Incoming enemy missiles have
stronger heat while boosting and weaker heat afterwards, so IR interception
is possible at shorter distances. Heat locks do not reveal identity.

Stock: 6 ready + 6 reserve IR missiles, separate from the existing 18 radar
missiles. Only an empty IR launcher reloads, in 60 simulation seconds. Selecting
L1/L2/L3 switches back to the corresponding radar launcher. Fired radar missiles
still need their existing radar lock even while the UI is in IR mode.

The UI shows cooling/acquisition/lock status, a seeker reticle, heat strength,
IR ammo and reload time. SOUND in the pause menu enables short search pulses
and a faster lock tone. The launcher seeker times out after 30 seconds.
Pause and app background freeze simulation timers and stop seeker tones.

Aircraft have limited fictional flare bursts and may react to nearby visible
IR missile approaches. Flares are temporary moving heat sources; they can
interrupt acquisition or distract an in-flight seeker if inside its view and
stronger than the aircraft. A missile drawn to a flare can miss. Missiles also
lose guidance through terrain or when a target moves outside the seeker cone.

This is an arcade game interpretation, not an exact War Thunder recreation or
validated real-weapon model. Values, loadouts, reaction probabilities, movement
and collision tolerances are gameplay choices. Search radar still provides
initial contact selection; this update does not add a standalone IRST search
screen, sun/cloud modeling, manual optical aiming, or advanced IRCCM.

Mechanics references:

Verification: deterministic acquisition, no radar warning/channel use, aspect,
terrain/ceiling, post-launch independence, flare diversion, cone loss, hot
missile interception, separate ammunition, reload/pause, timeout and reset.
Existing game and missile-interception tests plus seeded mission simulations
pass. Actual drawing code was checked with desktop Android stubs. Android
hardware playback, installation, touch controls and player balance need testing.
