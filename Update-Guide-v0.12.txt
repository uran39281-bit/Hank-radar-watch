TARGET TRACKING AND LOCK SYMBOLS
TRACK: dashed outline surrounding the target icon, TWS-style tracking.
LOCK: solid outline surrounding the target, with an additional star-shaped marker, hard lock for weapons requiring it.
Keep target icons visible inside both symbols and preserve identification colors.

MISSILE FIRING REQUIREMENTS
Active Homing: fire at tracked or locked targets. Before onboard seeker acquisition, updates use available battery datalink channels. After acquisition, guidance is independent.
Semi-Active Homing: cannot fire on tracking alone; establish and maintain hard lock. Losing lock interrupts guidance.

CONTROLS AND FEEDBACK
TRACK begins tracking and displays a dashed outline.
LOCK on a tracked target replaces it with a solid outline and star.
Releasing lock returns to TRACK if tracking remains available.
Losing tracking and lock removes both markers.
Disable FIRE when requirements are unmet and show LOCK REQUIRED, OUT OF RANGE, or NO DATALINK CHANNEL AVAILABLE as appropriate.
AI must recognize tracking and hard locking as separate events; tracking must not automatically produce a hard-lock warning.
