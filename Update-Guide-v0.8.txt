Additional Radar and Missile Mechanics

Add configurable characteristics for each radar system and missile. These values should determine their detection, tracking, guidance, and engagement capabilities.

Radar System Characteristics
- System name
- Maximum detection range: Farthest distance at which the radar can detect a contact.
- Maximum tracking range: Farthest distance at which the radar can maintain a target track.
- Maximum radar lock range: Farthest distance at which the radar can lock a target for engagement.
- Maximum simultaneous tracks: Number of targets the radar can track at once.
- Datalink channels: Number of available channels for exchanging target or guidance data.
- Radar scan speed: How quickly the radar scans and updates contact information.
- Infrared guidance support: Whether the system supports compatible IR-guided missiles.
- Maximum IR lock range: Farthest distance at which a compatible infrared seeker can acquire a target.

Missile Characteristics
- Missile name
- Guidance type: Radar, infrared, command guidance, or another method.
- Radar guidance mode: Active, semi-active, or passive, where applicable.
- Maximum guidance time: How long the missile can remain guided.
- Maximum speed
- Maximum G load: Maximum maneuvering acceleration.
- Mass
- Maximum angle of attack: Maximum AOA during flight.
- Maximum thrust: Maximum force produced by the missile's motor.

Use these characteristics to determine missile acceleration, turning ability, energy loss, and interception performance.
