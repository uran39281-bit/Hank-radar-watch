Air Defame 101 — Gameplay and UI Update

1. Intercept Incoming Missiles

Add the ability to shoot down enemy air-to-ground missiles, including anti-radiation missiles attacking the radar.

Detected incoming missiles must be selectable. The player should be able to use TRACK → LOCK → FIRE to engage them. Update NEXT TARGET to cycle through both aircraft and incoming missiles.

2. Target Identification Colors

Apply these colors to aircraft and missile contacts:

Identification state| Color| Meaning
UNKNOWN TARGET| Gray| The radar does not know whether the contact is friendly or enemy, and its type is unknown.
POSITIVE ID — ENEMY| Red| The radar has identified the contact as an enemy and knows its aircraft or missile type.
POSITIVE ID — FRIENDLY| Green| The radar has identified the contact as friendly and knows its aircraft or missile type. Reserve this state for future use.
UNSURE ID| Orange| The radar has identified the contact as an enemy, but its aircraft or missile type remains uncertain.

Update contact colors as identification changes. Show a text label alongside the color so the identification state is always clear.

Keep the surrounding interface primarily terminal green and white.

3. Move the Control Buttons

Move the gameplay controls from the bottom of the screen to a vertical panel on the right side:

- NEXT TARGET
- TRACK
- LOCK
- FIRE
- RANGE LIMIT

Keep the buttons large enough for comfortable touchscreen use.

4. Move the Priority Target List

Place PRIORITY TARGETS on the left side, directly below TARGET ANALYSIS.

5. Simplify the Top Bar

- Make the TIME, GUIDE, and PAUSE controls smaller.
- Remove the game title from the gameplay screen to free up space.
- Remove the radar-range readout from the top bar. Display it with the RANGE LIMIT control instead.
- Remove the visible mission timer.

6. Limit Base Survival

For now, give the player’s base a four-missile-hit limit. Display the remaining hits clearly and update the indicator after each confirmed impact.

7. Main Menu Background

Add an animated radar scan as the main menu background.

Blur and dim the background so the game title and PLAY button remain sharp, readable, and easy to see.
